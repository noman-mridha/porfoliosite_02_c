Published Research

AI-Driven Demand Forecasting in Bangladesh's FMCG Sector

Challenge: Bangladesh FMCG companies face significant inventory inefficiencies and forecasting challenges.

Solution: Conducted mixed-methods research (n=100) analyzing AI/ML adoption barriers and use-cases. Identified 35% adoption rate with key barriers: Data Quality (RII 0.87), Cost (0.81), Skills Gap (0.78). Top use-cases: Market Forecasting (0.82) and Logistics Optimization (0.76).

Impact: Received 80% ACSCP™ Scholarship for research excellence. Published in Supply Chain Insider (Vol. 16).
